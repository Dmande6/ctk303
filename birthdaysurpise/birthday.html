BIRTHDAY SONG

On the week of December 3rd 2003, Stand Up by Ludacris was the number one song in the world. I mean look at him in the back right there. A face any mother would love.

"Stand Up" is a song by Ludacris, released as the second single from his fourth album, Chicken-n-Beer, on August 19, 2003. It became his first song to peak atop the Billboard Hot 100—remaining on the chart for 28 weeks—on the week dated December 6 of that year. It contains a guest appearance from American rapper Shawnna, who provides a call-and-response dichotomy with Ludacris for the song's chorus.
