Birthday Song Throwback

December 2003 Flashback

On the week of December 3rd, 2003, “Stand Up” by Ludacris was the number one song in the world.

The track was released as the second single from his fourth album on August 19, 2003. It became his first song to reach number one on the Billboard Hot 100.

The song also features rapper Shawnna, who delivers the call-and-response chorus alongside Ludacris.
